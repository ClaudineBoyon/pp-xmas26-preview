  1. Memorial ornament · $14.95 (Hero · emotional keepsake)

    The only ornament in our Google Bestsellers (last 7 days) list (#5), and #7 in Best Sellers. Competitors (GeckoCustom, Pawsome Parents) have run "first Christmas" ornament ads since October.
    Gift: Marks a new puppy's or kitten's first Christmas. An easy gift for new pet parents.

    "Dog christmas ornament" is the most-searched term (Trends peak 100). About 87 active Meta ads for "pet ornament". Ornaments are carried by 9 of 14 competitors at $15–25.

    "Dog christmas stocking" is the #2 search term (peak 54). ModPaws' photo stocking has 177 reviews at $49.99. Nobody runs pet-stocking ads, so it works as an add-on to the ornament.
    Gift: Gives the pet their own place on the mantel. Pairs with an ornament as a set.
  5. In Memory suncatcher · from $29.95 (Premium keepsake)
    Memorial demand runs all year ("custom pet memorial gifts" +140%). Cuddle Clones has run memorial ads since September. It gives the collection a higher-AOV option.
    Gift: A larger remembrance gift that hangs in a window all year, not only at Christmas.
  6. Memorial night light · from $24.95 (Additional personalized gift)
    A mid-price step between the $14.95 ornament and the suncatcher. Listed on 9 Nov, so treat it as a test item with no track record.
    Gift: A bedside keepsake with their name and years. A sympathy gift that feels substantial.
