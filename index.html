Questions, answered

When will it arrive?

Delivered in 5–7 days if you order today. Every ornament is printed just for you in the USA after you order, so the sooner you order, the more time you have before Christmas. If production ever runs late, we email you before the stated time is up, and you can cancel for a full refund.

What kind of photo works best?

A sharp, well-lit photo works best, ideally taken in daylight without flash, with their face clearly visible. The ornament is printed exactly as your live preview shows, so if the preview looks dark or blurry, try another photo.

Does someone check my design before it's printed?

No. Your ornament is produced exactly as shown in your live preview, with no extra review step. That's why production can start right away. Please check the photo, name and spelling carefully before you order.

Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

No. We start preparing your personalized order as soon as you place it, so it can't be changed or cancelled afterwards. That includes the delivery address. Please double-check everything at checkout.

What if it arrives damaged, misprinted or not at all?

Our 100% Satisfaction Guarantee covers it. If your ornament arrives damaged, is misprinted or defective, is the wrong item, or hasn't arrived 15 business days after shipping, we send a free reprint. If a reprint isn't possible, you get a full refund. Email a photo within 30 days of delivery. You don't need to send anything back.

Can I return it if I change my mind?

Because each ornament is made just for you from your photo, we can't accept change-of-mind returns or exchanges. The guarantee above still covers misprints, damage and lost parcels.

Can I send it straight to the person I'm giving it to?

Yes. Enter their address as the delivery address at checkout. We ship to US addresses. Orders can't be changed once placed, so check the address carefully. If there's a problem with the ornament, the recipient can contact us too, using the order number or your name and email address.

How do the 2- and 3-ornament prices work?

Choose 2 or 3 in the quantity selector on the product page: 2 cost $26.92 (save 10%) and 3 cost $35.88 (save 20%). Shipping is $6.95 for one item and $9.95 for two or more. Sales tax is calculated at checkout.
